TITLE
Tiny Truck Wars: AI vs AI on the River Crossing (tech demo, 3 minutes uncut)


DESCRIPTION
Three minutes of the computer playing itself in Tiny Truck Wars, a small RTS I've been building in Godot where most of the work went into the ground rather than the armies.

Both sides start with a garage, two fuel depots and a few tanks and trucks, on a map split by a river you can only cross at two fords. Watch the fields between the bases: every vehicle leaves ruts, mud gets pushed into berms, shells leave craters, and the ground stays churned up for the rest of the match. That ground slows the next vehicle down and makes it burn more fuel, so the fords turn into bogs as the fight goes back and forth across them.

Things to look for:
- Tanks firing shells on an arc, leading their targets
- Helicopters getting pushed around by the wind
- A hippy van throwing a smoke screen to cover a crossing
- Fuel depots going up in chain reactions
- Wrecks burning and leaking oil

The footage is straight from the game, recorded at 1080p and 60 fps with the camera following the fighting. There's no sound yet.

It's a tech demo: placeholder art, a simple computer opponent and rough balance. You can play it in the browser or download it for Windows and Linux:
https://ssube.itch.io/tiny-truck-wars

The source is on GitHub (code MIT, art CC BY-NC 4.0):
